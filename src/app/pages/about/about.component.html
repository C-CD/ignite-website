About Us

Ignite Megastars, established by C&CD Consulting Limited, is a platform dedicated to discovering, nurturing, and promoting grassroot talents across various sports.

Designed as a rigorous evaluation platform, through competitions, Ignite Megastars is committed to maintaining integrity and transparency in sports talent identification and selection for youths between ages 16 to 19, allowing top talents to be discovered.

Beginning with football- Ignite Soccer Stars, will provide the opportunity to both male and female athletes to showcase their talent and rise to stardom, by partaking in the Ignite competitions and undergoing assessment through a transparent system of voting by Judges, Coaches and fans. Participation is completely free

With our platform, coaches and football fans can assess players performance by voting for them based on their performance in the competitions. Winners will be rewarded with scholarships to both local and international academies.

In addition to building the technical skills of these athletes, the program includes learning curriculums in ethics, communication and interpersonal skills for participants.

Ignite Megastars is the first platform of its kind, incorporating the Ignite Management and Mentoring Academy (IMMA), which supports all tournament participants throughout their career journey to help them build a rewarding future.

This innovative project has received endorsements from the Lagos State Football Association (LSFA).

Vision

Changing lives by discovering and promoting sports talent.

Mission

Creating a platform for recognizing and developing grassroots sports talent, with the goal of cultivating a vast pool of world-class athletes in the country.

Unique Value Proposition
  • Access

    Ignite Megastars offers young athletes aspiring to pursue professional sports, a structured platform for their talent to be recognized and developed. The program also includes players learning curriculums in ethics, communication and interpersonal skills. Winners will be rewarded with scholarships to both local and international academies.

  • Voting Model

    The platform provides an opportunity to both male and female athletes to showcase their talent, by partaking in the Ignite competitions and undergoing assessment through a transparent system of voting by Judges, Coaches and fans.

    The Ignite Megastars competition is poised to become the nation's premier sports talent discovery program. With its innovative voting system, the public plays an active role in choosing the next generation of sports stars. This model fosters strong audience engagement and the long-term sustainability of Ignite Megastars.

  • Youth Development

    Alongside the competitions, top-performing athletes will be introduced to the "Mentoring Academy," an initiative where they will receive guidance to develop both their professional career and personal growth.

    The Ignite Management and Mentoring Academy (IMMA), also supports all participants in the competition through their career journey to help them build a rewarding future.

  • Coaching Academy

    In addition to the competitions, top performing athletes will be introduced to the “Ignite Coaching Academy” which will help them nurture their skills both as athletes and individuals. Ignite will provide opportunities for these youths to participate in various competitions locally and internationally, as well as provide mentorship.
